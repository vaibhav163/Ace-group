About Ace Group

Ace Group is a Noida-based real estate developer led by Ajay Chaudhary. The group has built its name on large residential townships and commercial projects along the Noida Expressway, the Yamuna Expressway and in Greater Noida West — the three corridors shaping the region’s next decade of growth.

Its residential portfolio ranges from golf-facing residences in low-density Sector 150 (Parkway, Golfshire and the new ACE Arte by Ar. Hafeez Contractor) and expressway apartments (Starlit, ACE–Mahagun Medalleo) to neoclassical towers on the Yamuna Expressway (Verde, Terra), ultra-luxury homes in Greater Noida West (Han’ei, alongside delivered Divino, Platinum, City and Aspire), plotted townships (Acreville, Estate) and low-rise floors in Gurugram (Palm Floors). On the commercial side, ACE Capitol brings ready office spaces on lease to Sector 132 and ACE Studio to Sector 126, while ACE153, Medley Avenue, City Square, YXP, Hive and Edit cover retail, offices and business suites.
